### Introduction and Definition
This Privacy Policy sets out how we, Recall, use and protect your personal data that you provide to us, or that is otherwise obtained or generated in connection with your use of Recall (the “Services”). For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to Recall, and “you” refers to you, the user of the Services.

### Principles
We follow simple principles when it comes to your data:
- We do not collect personal data
- We do not track users
- We do not share data with third parties
- Any purchases are handled entirely by Apple

### Information you provide
**None**. Recall does not collect, store, or process any personal information.

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple using your Apple ID. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any personal data associated with purchases.

### How we share your information
We do not share any information at any time.

### Third-Party Services
Recall uses Apple’s in-app purchase system to provide subscriptions. Any data related to purchases is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple’s Privacy Policy.
