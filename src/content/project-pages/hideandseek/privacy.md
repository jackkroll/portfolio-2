### Introduction and Definition
This Privacy Policy sets out how we, hide & seek, use and protect your personal data that you provide to us, or that is otherwise obtained or generated by us, in connection with your use of hide & seek, (the “Services”). For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, ‘we’, ‘us’ and ‘our’ refers to hide & seek, and ‘you’ refers to you, the user of the Services.

### Principles
We follow simple principles when it comes to your data:
- Your data is used exactly as you expect
- Your data is not shared with anyone, at any time
- Your data does not leave your device without your explicit consent

### Information you provide

**Camera access**. Information from your camera is only used on device and is only used to participate in the game, it is never sent to us or any 3rd party at any time.
**Location access**. Your location is used at the core of the experience, it is only accessed when you are actively playing and only send to your friends during the game. No information is ever sent to us or any 3rd parties
**Managing your information**. All information used by hide & seek is stored on-device, and can be managed through hide & seek’s application Settings, or through Apple's system controls.

### How we share your information
We do not share any information at any time.

### Updates
This privacy policy will be updated as needed so that it is accurate, current, and clear.
